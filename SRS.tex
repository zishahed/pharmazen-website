\sectionbadge{1}{Introduction}
\section{Introduction}

This document establishes the complete Software Requirements Specification (SRS) for the \textbf{PharmaZen} website — an online pharmacy and health-product platform. It defines all functional and non-functional requirements to guide the development, testing, and delivery lifecycle.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Purpose}

The purpose of this SRS is to provide a detailed and agreed-upon description of the PharmaZen website system. It serves as the foundational reference for all project stakeholders — including developers, designers, QA engineers, and business owners — ensuring a shared understanding of what the system must accomplish.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Intended Audience}

This document is intended for the following parties:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item \textbf{Development Team} — Front-end and back-end engineers responsible for building the system
  \item \textbf{UI/UX Designers} — Responsible for designing user interfaces that satisfy the stated requirements
  \item \textbf{QA Engineers} — Responsible for validating implemented features against this specification
  \item \textbf{Project Managers} — Overseeing schedule, scope, and delivery milestones
  \item \textbf{Business Stakeholders} — PharmaZen owners and executives reviewing product direction
  \item \textbf{Regulatory Reviewers} — Ensuring compliance with pharmaceutical and data regulations
\end{itemize}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Intended Use}

This SRS shall be used as:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item A contractual reference between the development team and business stakeholders
  \item A baseline for test plan development and acceptance criteria
  \item A guide for identifying and managing change requests throughout the project lifecycle
  \item A reference document for onboarding new team members
\end{itemize}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Product Scope}

PharmaZen is a comprehensive e-commerce website specializing in pharmaceutical products, health supplements, personal care items, and related medical goods. The platform will enable customers to browse products, upload prescriptions, place orders, and manage their health profiles.

Core capabilities of the platform include:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=4pt]
  \item Online pharmacy storefront with product catalog and search
  \item Prescription upload, verification, and management
  \item Secure user registration, authentication, and profile management
  \item Order placement, real-time tracking, and order history
  \item Admin dashboard for inventory, order, and user management
  \item Integration with bKash payment gateway
\end{itemize}

\sectionbadge{2}{Overall Description}
\section{Overall Description}

This section provides a high-level overview of the PharmaZen system, including its user base, operating environment, constraints, and assumptions that govern its design and development.

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{User Classes and Characteristics}

The PharmaZen platform serves four primary user classes, each with distinct access levels and interaction needs:

\vspace{8pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
\begin{tabular}{|L{3.2cm}|L{12cm}|}
  \hline
  \rowcolor{lightgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Guest Visitor} & \small Unauthenticated users who can browse the product catalog, view product details and prices, and create an account. Cannot place orders or upload prescriptions. \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{medgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Registered Customer} & \small Authenticated users who can manage profiles, upload prescriptions, place and track orders, access order history, and contact pharmacists. Primary revenue-generating user class. \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{lightgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Pharmacist / Staff} & \small Licensed healthcare professionals who verify prescriptions and oversee medication safety flags. \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{medgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}System Administrator} & \small Technical and business administrators who manage all platform operations including user accounts, inventory, promotions, reports, and system configurations. \\
  \hline
\end{tabular}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{User Needs}

Based on user class analysis, the following core needs have been identified:

\subsubsection{Registered Customers}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item Easily search, filter, and find pharmaceutical and health products
  \item Securely upload and manage prescriptions for prescribed medication orders
  \item Complete purchases quickly with bKash payment
  \item Track orders in real time from placement to delivery
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Pharmacists / Staff}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item Efficiently review and approve or reject uploaded prescriptions
  \item Flag medications that require counseling or special handling
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{System Administrators}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt]
  \item Manage product inventory levels, categories, and pricing
  \item Handle user disputes, returns, and policy enforcement
  \item View sales analytics reports
\end{itemize}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Operating Environment}

The PharmaZen website will operate in the following technical environment:

\vspace{8pt}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}
\begin{tabular}{|L{3.2cm}|L{12cm}|}
  \hline
  \rowcolor{lightgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Platform Type} & \small Web application accessible via modern desktop and mobile browsers \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{medgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Frontend} & \small React.js (Vite) — responsive single-page application targeting screens from 320px to 2560px wide \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{lightgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Backend} & \small Node.js with Express.js REST API, or equivalent server-side framework \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{medgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Database} & \small PostgreSQL hosted on Neon Tech (serverless) \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{lightgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Cloud Hosting} & \small Vercel (frontend and serverless functions); automatic CI/CD from Git repository \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{medgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}CDN} & \small Vercel Edge Network for static assets, images, and global performance \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{lightgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Browser Support} & \small Chrome 100+, Firefox 100+, Safari 15+, Edge 100+ on desktop and mobile \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{medgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}Payment Gateway} & \small Create mock bkash server for token pass testing, actual Sandbox requires trade license \\
  \hline
  \rowcolor{lightgreen}\textbf{\small\color{darkgreen}SSL/TLS} & \small HTTPS enforced across all pages and API endpoints \\
  \hline
\end{tabular}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Constraints}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item \textbf{Regulatory Compliance:} The platform must comply with applicable pharmaceutical regulations and digital health guidelines in the operating jurisdiction.
  \item \textbf{Data Privacy:} All user health and personal data must be handled in accordance with applicable data protection regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA or local equivalents).
  \item \textbf{Prescription Verification:} Medications classified as prescription-only must not be dispensed without a valid, verified prescription from a licensed pharmacist.
  \item \textbf{Payment Security:} All payment processing must be handled securely via the bKash payment gateway.
  \item \textbf{Technology Stack:} The development team is constrained to use the agreed-upon technology stack to ensure maintainability and team expertise alignment.
  \item \textbf{Budget \& Timeline:} Development must be delivered within the agreed project budget and timeline as defined in the project charter.
  \item \textbf{Third-Party APIs:} Features relying on external APIs (e.g., delivery tracking, payment) are subject to the availability and terms of those third-party providers.
\end{itemize}

% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsection{Assumptions}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=5pt]
  \item All users accessing the platform are assumed to have access to a stable internet connection.
  \item The platform will initially target a single jurisdiction/country; multi-country expansion is out of scope for v1.0.
  \item Licensed pharmacists reviewing prescriptions will be pre-vetted and onboarded by PharmaZen management.
  \item Payment gateway providers will maintain their service agreements and uptime SLAs.
  \item Product catalog and inventory data will be initially seeded by the PharmaZen operations team.
  \item Users are responsible for providing accurate health and prescription information; the platform is not liable for fraudulent submissions.
  \item Third-party delivery partners will be responsible for last-mile logistics, and PharmaZen will integrate only with their tracking APIs.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Non-Functional Requirements}

Non-functional requirements define the quality attributes and operational constraints the PharmaZen system must satisfy.

% ─ 3.2.1 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsubsection{Performance}

\reqtablestart
\rid{NFR-01} & \textbf{\small Page Load Time} & \small All public-facing pages must load within 3 seconds under normal load conditions (up to 500 concurrent users). & \phigh \\
\hline
\rowcolor{rowgray}\rid{NFR-02} & \textbf{\small Search Response} & \small Product search queries must return results within 2 seconds for 95\% of requests. & \phigh \\
\hline
\rid{NFR-03} & \textbf{\small Checkout Throughput} & \small The checkout process must support at least 100 concurrent transactions without degradation in response time. & \phigh \\
\hline
\rowcolor{rowgray}\rid{NFR-04} & \textbf{\small Scalability} & \small The system must horizontally scale to support up to 10,000 concurrent users during peak periods (e.g., promotional events). & \pmed \\
\hline
\reqtableend

% ─ 3.2.2 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\subsubsection{Security}

\reqtablestart
\rid{NFR-05} & \textbf{\small Data Encryption} & \small All sensitive data (passwords, health records, payment info) must be encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). & \phigh \\
\hline
\rowcolor{rowgray}\rid{NFR-06} & \textbf{\small Auth Security} & \small Passwords must be hashed using bcrypt (minimum cost factor 12). Plain-text passwords must never be stored. & \phigh \\
\hline
\rid{NFR-07} & \textbf{\small Session Management} & \small User sessions must expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. JWT tokens must have a maximum lifespan of 24 hours. & \phigh \\
\hline
\rowcolor{rowgray}\rid{NFR-08} & \textbf{\small Input Validation} & \small All user inputs must be validated and sanitized server-side to prevent SQL injection, XSS, and CSRF attacks. & \phigh \\
\hline
\rowcolor{rowgray}\rid{NFR-09} & \textbf{\small Vulnerability Scan} & \small The platform must undergo automated vulnerability scans (OWASP Top 10) before each major release. & \pmed \\
\hline
\reqtableend

\subsubsection{Reliability \& Availability}

\reqtablestart
\rid{NFR-11} & \textbf{\small Uptime SLA} & \small The platform must maintain a minimum uptime of 99.5\% measured on a monthly basis (excluding scheduled maintenance windows). & \phigh \\
\hline
\reqtableend
